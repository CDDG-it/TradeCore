## Generation prompts

All three prompts requested mature hand-painted 2D anime background art, visible ink lines, watercolor or gouache grain, deep navy `#0B1120`, restrained turquoise `#14B8A6` and cyan `#06B6D4`, and no text, logos, holograms, 3D rendering, or glossy tech effects.

1. **Hero:** A quiet trading workspace at blue dawn, with one laptop showing a simple candlestick chart, notebook, pen, tea, and a city window. Place the scene on the right and leave dark space on the left for an HTML headline.
2. **Trade plan:** A close overhead view of an open paper journal with a hand-drawn candlestick sketch, blank checklist, empty ruled lines, and a pencil on a dark wooden desk.
3. **Session review:** A calm trader seen from behind, reviewing a laptop chart and an open paper journal by a city window and small desk lamp.
